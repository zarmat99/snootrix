# SNOOTRIX Face Editor

Draw faces for a protogen LED visor (two 64×32 HUB75 panels or a chain of MAX7219 8×8 modules), animate them (Blink, Talk and Boop make the frames for you, or draw frame by frame), watch them live on a 3D head, talk and see the mouth move, boop the snoot, and export them for your firmware (PNG, BMP, animated GIF, C arrays, MAX7219 bytes) or share them as a link or an image.

**Open it: https://zarmat99.github.io/snootrix/**

- Free, no account.
- Everything runs in your browser: your drawings and saved faces stay on your device, and the microphone is analysed on your device and never recorded or sent anywhere. No cookies, no account.
- Visits are counted anonymously with Umami (EU servers, no cookies, no personal data), only to know how many people use the editor. Open the page once with `?notrack` at the end of the address to switch it off on your device.
- The waitlist buttons open a Tally form (tally.so) in a popup over the editor. It loads only when you tap one of them, and what you type there is handled by Tally, not by this page. The editor itself collects no personal data.
- Heads: the SNOOTRIX concept head is an original design, the same as in the SNOOTRIX video, made in code and Blender (no AI-generated images). The M16 mk3 head is "Protogen M16 mk3" by M16 Studios (https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4894173), licensed CC BY 4.0, simplified for the web, with the HUB75 panels and the MAX7219 matrices placed where its design puts them. The M16 mk2 head is "Protogen M16 mk2" V1.6.0 by M16 Studios (https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4865223), licensed CC BY 4.0, simplified for the web, with its 11 MAX7219 matrices placed where its design puts them. Credits are on the page and in `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt`.

SNOOTRIX is a plug & play LED visor controller in development. This editor is meant to become the face editor of the real board. Waitlist: https://tally.so/r/eqdb1k?src=github

This repository holds the built site, published with GitHub Pages. Third-party code (three.js and the meshoptimizer decoder, both MIT) is listed in `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt`.
