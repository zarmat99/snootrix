About

Made by Matteo, an independent maker in Italy. SNOOTRIX is a prototype: nothing is for sale yet.

The SNOOTRIX concept head and the faces are original designs. “Protogen M16 mk3” and “Protogen M16 mk2” V1.6.0 by M16 Studios, CC BY 4.0: simplified for the web, panels and modules added.

Privacy: everything runs in your browser and stays on this device; the microphone is never recorded. Visits are counted anonymously (Umami, EU, no cookies; ?notrack turns it off). The waitlist is a Tally form, loaded only when you open it.
