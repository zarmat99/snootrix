3D model: "Protogen M16 mk3" V1.2.2 by M16 Studios
https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4894173
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes made for this editor: the assembled parts were merged by material,
simplified to about 74,000 triangles and scaled; the mannequin head, the visor
moulds, the 8x8 matrix frame and modules were left out. The two LED panels are
drawn where the design's "matrix L/R" parts are (192 x 96 mm, two HUB75 P3
panels), with the face shown live on them.
