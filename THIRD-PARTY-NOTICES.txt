3D model: "Protogen M16 mk3" V1.2.2 by M16 Studios
https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4894173
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes made for this editor: the assembled parts were merged by material,
simplified to about 74,000 triangles and scaled; the mannequin head, the visor
moulds, the 8x8 matrix frame and modules were left out. The two LED panels are
drawn where the design's "matrix L/R" parts are (192 x 96 mm, two HUB75 P3
panels), with the face shown live on them.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3D model: "Protogen M16 mk2" V1.6.0 by M16 Studios
https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4865223
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes made for this editor: the one-piece base (FRAME_COMBINED), the front
frame (FRONT_FRAME) and the ear rings (FRAME_OVERLAY) were placed together with
the screw holes of the ear rings, the base was cut at the middle and mirrored to
fit the front frame, the visor was made from the outer surface of the visor mould
(MASTER_MODEL_2, 2 mm thick, trimmed around the ear rings and under the base
rim), dark discs were added in the ear rings, and everything was simplified to
about 67,000 triangles and scaled. The 11 MAX7219 8x8 modules (3 for the mouth
and 2 for the eye on each cheek, 1 nose) are drawn where the design places them,
with the face shown live on them.
